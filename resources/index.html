About us

Kourtney Kovanis is the Director of Content at Tendo Communications, a content and UX agency. She’s the mom of a super energetic toddler and super lethargic senior dog. Get in touch: kourtney.kovanis@gmail.com

Kayleen Schreiber is an information design consultant and science communicator. But most of her time is spent as chauffeur, butler, and chef to two young children. Get in touch: kayleen.e.schreiber@gmail.com

In putting together this project, Kourtney and Kayleen met several times with a group of like-minded ladies to discuss mom rage, irritability, general frustrations…but also how helpful it is to discuss marriage, family, life struggles with a group that is both encouraging and genuine. We worked together on the anger tracker for two weeks, exposing our more vulnerable moments for the sake of data and insights into whether there were commonalities to our anger outbursts. We learned a lot from each other and grew closer as friends and sisters in Christ.
